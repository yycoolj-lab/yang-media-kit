01原創靜脈技術與國際認可

兩項術式皆由楊醫師獨立提出，2026 年以單一作者發表於 Journal of Vascular Surgery Cases, Innovations and Techniques。

SPOT
Segmental Point Obliteration Technique
分段點狀閉合術
解決的問題
大隱靜脈逆流延伸到膝下時，指引建議全段治療，但膝下段緊貼隱神經，連續式雷射消融容易造成術後麻木。
做法
改變能量給予的方式，以分段點狀閉合靜脈，保留神經。
首次報告
19 例術後一週無感覺異常。
Segmental point obliteration technique for nerve-sparing endovenous laser ablation of the below-knee great saphenous vein（2026）
UFLA
Sheathless Ultra-Fine Laser Ablation
無鞘超細雷射消融術
解決的問題
標準雷射消融需要 5F 或 6F 導管鞘，短段的深部肌肉分支與穿通枝無法置入；軟組織腫瘤切除或外傷後的困難解剖尤其棘手。
做法
不使用導管鞘，以超細光纖直接消融短段逆流分支。
The sheathless ultrafine laser ablation technique for managing deep muscle branch reflux in hostile anatomy following rhabdomyosarcoma resection（2026）

01Original venous techniques and international recognition

Dr. Yang developed both techniques independently and published them in 2026 as sole author in the Journal of Vascular Surgery Cases, Innovations and Techniques.

SPOT
Segmental Point Obliteration Technique
THE PROBLEM
When great saphenous vein reflux extends below the knee, guidelines recommend treating the full length. The below-knee segment runs alongside the saphenous nerve, and continuous laser ablation often leaves numbness.
THE APPROACH
Change how the energy is delivered: close the vein at separate points along the segment and spare the nerve.
FIRST REPORT
No paraesthesia at one week in 19 cases.
Segmental point obliteration technique for nerve-sparing endovenous laser ablation of the below-knee great saphenous vein (2026)
UFLA
Sheathless Ultra-Fine Laser Ablation
THE PROBLEM
Standard laser ablation needs a 5F or 6F introducer sheath, which cannot be placed in short deep muscle branches and perforators. Anatomy altered by soft tissue tumour resection or trauma is especially difficult.
THE APPROACH
Ablate the short refluxing branch directly with an ultra-fine fibre, without a sheath.
The sheathless ultrafine laser ablation technique for managing deep muscle branch reflux in hostile anatomy following rhabdomyosarcoma resection (2026)
